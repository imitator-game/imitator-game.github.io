How it works

Four steps, about a day of compute

  1. 1

    Train on a fixed corpus

    Pretrain on the 15-, 30-, or 45-task IG-10K split. The splits are frozen and shipped with the harness, so a corpus size is comparable across every entry on the board.

    ig train --corpus 45 --policy your_policy
  2. 2

    Roll out all four regimes

    Seen tasks, zero-shot on the held-out five, from-scratch few-shot, and pretrain + fine-tune. Every regime runs at all four levels — 40 task–level pairs per domain.

    ig eval --regimes seen,zs,scratch,pft --levels L0-L3
  3. 3

    Package the rollouts

    The harness writes a bundle with per-episode videos, the automated metrics, and a metadata file describing the model. Videos are what the Arena replays, so keep them.

    ig package --out my_model_bundle/
  4. 4

    Send it in

    Open a submission below. We verify the metrics reproduce, add the entry to the leaderboard, and enter the rollouts into the Arena rotation for human comparison.

    ≈ 3–5 working days to verify
What counts

Rules of the board

The held-out five stay held out

No unseen-task demonstrations in pretraining. Few-shot regimes use exactly 10 demonstrations per unseen task — the same budget for every level.

One human video in, one rollout out

The policy sees the demonstration video for the episode it is executing. No privileged state, no per-episode hand tuning, no retries that are not reported.

Declare the corpus and the regime

Entries are listed with their pretraining corpus size and training regime. A 45-task entry is not compared against a 15-task entry without saying so.

Rollouts are public

Submitted videos go into the Gallery and the Arena. Weights are yours; the behaviour is on the record so anyone can see what the number means.

Bundle format

What to send

A directory, or a link to one. The harness produces this layout for you; if you evaluated outside the harness, match it by hand.

Directory
my_model_bundle/
├── meta.json              model card and training setup
├── metrics.json           SR / Sub-SR per task × level × regime
├── rollouts/
│   ├── seen/L0/<task>/ep_000.mp4
│   ├── zs/L3/<task>/ep_004.mp4
│   └── …                  one clip per evaluated episode
└── logs/                  harness stdout, config, git SHA

  "authors": ["…"],
  "paper_url": "",

  "license": "…",
  "harness_version": "1.0.0"
}
